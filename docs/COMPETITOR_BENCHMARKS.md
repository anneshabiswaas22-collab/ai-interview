## Feature benchmark worksheet

For each feature, record:
- Product and **exact public URL**
- Observation date and version
- Verifiable evidence (screenshots/official documentation)
- Availability tier, geographic restrictions and pricing
- UX quality, latency, correctness or workflow depth (measured, not inferred)
- Our target behavior, implementation effort and evaluation method

Do not use terms like "market #1", "best", or "feature parity achieved" without defined methodology and actual verification.

## Questions to answer during discovery

1. What does a compelling candidate dashboard include beyond interview history?
2. How does each product prepare personalized questions from candidate context?
3. How do real-time voice products deal with silence, interruption and changing network conditions?
4. How is coding graded? Which languages, tests, time limits and anti-cheat controls are used?
5. How are system-design drawings saved and discussed?
6. What are the qualifications and limitations of their scoring?
7. What features are plan-gated? How much does a completed interview cost?
8. What accessibility alternatives are available for speaking/typing?
9. Which parts are true implementations vs demos or marketing language?
10. Which product strengths can we improve without copying protected code or content?

## Competitive positioning hypothesis

We win by delivering **more reliable and insightful practice per dollar**, with self-hostable models, trustworthy evidence-based feedback and a coherent UX. Feature count alone is not a defensible advantage. A measured benchmark suite and repeated user testing determine priority.
